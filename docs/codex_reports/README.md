# Codex Report Lifecycle

Files in this directory are task-time, append-only evidence reports. Preserve
their measured results, exact dispositions, validation records, and scoped
claim boundaries. Do not silently rewrite a historical result to match a later
route.

Words such as “current”, “active”, “next”, “authorized”, and “blocked” inside a
report describe the task closeout at that time. They expire as live routing
when the task ends. Determine present status and permission through the
applicable `AGENTS.md` chain, `CODEX_CONTEXT.md`,
`docs/research_strategy/active_phase.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, and
`NEXT_ACTIONS.md`.

Reports are internal evidence and reasoning surfaces, not primary literature.
Verify scientific facts and citations against original sources, and route
claim status through `docs/paper/final_claim_matrix.md`. A report may document a
precise historical NO-GO, but it cannot by itself reopen a route, create a
family-wide prohibition, or authorize a new experiment.
